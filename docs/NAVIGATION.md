# Navigation

Goal: in any space you always know where to go. Four destinations, everything else one tap away under **More**.

## Before (18 screens, 8 tabs in Films + 4 global links + 7 spaces)

| Where | Links |
|---|---|
| Films tabs (top bar / bottom bar showed the first 5) | Home · Swipe · Discover · My list · Taste · Tonight · Sagas · Graph |
| Other spaces | Home · Swipe · Discover · My list · Taste · (+ Add / Playlists / Trips) |
| Sidebar, always | Profile · 7 spaces · Sync status · Pick together · Second brain · Import & connect · Settings |
| Phone | Top bar (profile, space) · bottom bar: Home · Swipe · Discover · My list · Taste (Tonight, Sagas, Graph only through the space sheet) |

## After

| Destination | What's in it | Route |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Welcome steps (first visit) · Continue (series in progress, next in your sagas) · For you · **Tonight** (mood × length × your platforms) · Because you liked · Added by you | `/` |
| **Swipe** | The deck, one card at a time, with trailer / song preview | `/swipe/` |
| **Discover** | Search titles, people, themes · filters · sorting | `/discover/` |
| **My list** | Watchlist · In progress · Loved · Diary (everything you answered) · **Sagas** (films) | `/lists/` (`?tab=sagas`) |
| **More** | This space: My taste · Tonight full page · Taste graph (films) / Playlists (music) / Trips (places) / + Add · App: Pick together · Second brain · Import & connect · Settings (connections, sync, language) | menu (desktop top bar) / sheet (phone) |

Sidebar (desktop): profile · spaces · sync status · Settings. Phone: top bar (profile, space switcher) and a
bottom tab bar **Home · Swipe · Discover · My list · More**.

Old routes still work (`/tonight/`, `/sagas/`, `/graph/`, `/me/`…): they're just no longer in the main bar.
